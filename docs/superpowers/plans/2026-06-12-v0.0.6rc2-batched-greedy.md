**Goal:** Remove the v0.0.6rc1 Greedy sampling regression and make inactive Top-P settings explicit.

**Architecture:** Keep vocabulary-sharded LM Head output. For an all-Greedy batch, compute every row's local maximum together, pack maximum values and exact float32 token IDs into one tensor, and issue one AllGather per Decode step. Stochastic and mixed sampling retain the exact fallback path.

**Tech Stack:** Python, PyTorch distributed/HCCL, unittest.
